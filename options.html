Sites

How to use
  1. Add your instance URL below (e.g. gitlab.com). It is saved as soon as you leave the field.
  2. Allow access to the site if your browser asks.
  3. Go to your ticket (issue or merge request).
  4. In GitLab, click the "Time tracking" header in the sidebar to view the report.

Calendar

Show calendar events as placeholders in the "My day" timeline. Use your Google account, or paste a secret iCal address.

If checked, this overrides the iCal URL below and fetches events directly using your Google sign-in.
Google Calendar → Settings → your calendar → "Integrate calendar" → "Secret address in iCal format".

My day

Default time block shown in the "My day" timeline (24h, e.g. 8 to 17).

Use 0:00 to disable the daily target.
Off is safer when you share your screen.

Notifications

Hides the toolbar total, the end-of-day reminder and the GitLab sidebar toggle while you share your screen. Applies to this browser only.
Presentation mode is on.
The reminder is shown as a notification by your browser itself, so it follows your browser and system notification settings.
